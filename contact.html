02 / Useful

What makes an enquiry easy to answer

  • The decision you are trying to make, and the date it has to be made by.
  • Who has to be convinced: a board, an investment committee, a regulator, a customer.
  • What you have already tried, and what did not work.
  • Any constraint that is genuinely fixed. Budget, geography, existing contracts, incumbent technology.

A paragraph containing those four things gets a substantive reply, usually within a day.
